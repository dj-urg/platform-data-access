X (formerly Twitter)

Prepaid pay-per-use for everyone — and, after the first DSA non-compliance fine, a free researcher route that X must fix by early 2027.

What
X API v2 — now prepaid pay-per-use (subscriptions phased out during 2026). Separate free DSA researcher route for EU systemic-risk research.

The state of play

The free Academic Research track (10M tweets/month, full-archive search) was shut down in spring 2023 and nothing has replaced it. In early 2026, X closed its Free/Basic/Pro subscriptions to new signups, migrating existing subscribers to prepaid credits over the year: reads are billed per object returned (posts $0.005; user objects $0.010), writes per request. There is a hard ceiling of 3 million post reads per month outside Enterprise contracts. Set a spend cap in the developer console before running any collection.

The DSA route — after the €120 million fine

On 5 December 2025 the European Commission fined X €120 million — the first DSA non-compliance decision — partly for failing to give researchers access to public data (obstructive eligibility screening, inadequate review, overly limited quotas, and terms banning independent collection). In July 2026 the Commission accepted X's corrective plan: eligible researchers must get access free of charge, with timely processing and adequate volumes, and X's terms may no longer contractually bar eligible researchers from scraping publicly available data. Implementation is due by about January 2027, under an independent audit.

  1. For EU systemic-risk research, apply via the X DSA Researcher Application Form, linked from help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/dsa-vetted-researchers (open the page in a normal browser; it blocks automated access). Frame the proposal explicitly around DSA Art. 34/35 systemic risks (disinformation, elections, minors, mental health, illegal content, fundamental rights).
  2. For non-public data, use the Art. 40(4) portal route via BIPT.
  3. For small paid collections, create a developer account at developer.x.com, buy credits, and budget $5 (≈ €4.40) per 1,000 posts read.
  4. Document every interaction with X — response times and refusals are relevant evidence under the Commission's enhanced supervision.
