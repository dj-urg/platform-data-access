Chapter 4 · TikTok

TikTok Research API

Public videos, comments, profiles and follower networks — TikTok's DSA Art. 40(12) mechanism, free for eligible academics.

What
TikTok Research API + Virtual Compute Environment: public videos, comments, profiles, follower networks. TikTok's DSA Art. 40(12) mechanism.
Who
Academics at institutions in the EEA, US, UK, Canada or Switzerland; academic and not-for-profit institutions in Brazil (online youth-safety research); EU not-for-profit research organisations (usually via the compute environment only). PhD candidates need a supervisor's endorsement letter.
Cost
Free.
Timeline
Officially up to 4 weeks; in practice often longer.
Apply at
developers.tiktok.com/application/research-api
Docs
developers.tiktok.com/products/research-api

What data you get

Public videos (description, creation time, hashtags, music, view/like/comment/share counts), comments, user profiles (bio, follower counts), follower and following lists, liked/pinned/reposted videos, and EU TikTok Shop data. Full field list: Research API codebook. Note the freshness lags: new videos can take up to 48 hours to appear; statistics update within up to 10 days.

How to apply

  1. Create a TikTok for Developers account with your institutional email: developers.tiktok.com/signup.
  2. Submit the research application (link above) with: institutional details, a clearly defined research proposal, funding disclosure, evidence of ethics review (ethics committee approval), a data security plan, and named collaborators. PhD candidates attach the advisor endorsement letter (template on the application page).
  3. Review takes up to ~4 weeks officially (longer in practice). On approval the principal researcher manages the organisation account and can add up to 9 collaborators (each reviewed individually; collaborators at other institutions need their own application).
  4. Build against the getting-started guide; access tokens expire every 2 hours. Check the current Research API Terms — they require refreshing stored data at least every 15 days, deleting what has been removed from the platform.

Limits and caveats

  • Quota (shared by your whole lab/organisation): 1,000 requests and 100,000 records per day; 100 records per request; follower/following endpoints have a separate 2M records/day allowance. Daily reset 00:00 UTC. TikTok states it grants no exceptions.
  • Independent audits report substantial, unexplained gaps between what users see and what the API returns (e.g., an AI Forensics audit testing ~260,000 data-donated video URLs: Entrena-Serrano et al., 2025; Bekavac & Mayer, 2026). Treat completeness as an empirical question in your design.
  • Separate ad transparency route: the Commercial Content API (EU ads and commercial content; open to researchers and the public worldwide; approval typically within ~2 working days) and the public Commercial Content Library.
