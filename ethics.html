Chapter 10 · Ethics & GDPR

The pre-flight checklist

Start the ethics dossier before you apply anywhere — platforms ask for it, and the DSA portal requires a full GDPR safeguards description.

  • Before applying anywhere: platform research applications routinely require evidence of ethics review and a data security plan. Start the ethics dossier first — TikTok asks for it explicitly, and the DSA portal requires a full GDPR safeguards description.
  • VUB contacts: Data Protection Office — dpo@vub.be (DPIA support, data management plans); your faculty's ethics committee (for human-sciences research, ECHW) for the review platforms ask about.
  • GDPR toolbox for platform data: legal basis under Art. 6(1)(e)/(f); research safeguards under Art. 89 (minimisation, pseudonymisation); exemption from notifying each data subject where disproportionate (Art. 14(5)(b)); but a DPIA for large-scale collection.
  • Mind the AI clauses: Telegram and Reddit terms prohibit training AI/ML models on their data; TikTok's terms require refreshing/deleting stored data periodically. Check the live terms before building your pipeline.
  • De-identify by default: hash or remove usernames and IDs in publications and shared datasets; never collect group member lists unless strictly necessary.
  • Archive your access paperwork: approvals, terms versions and correspondence — journals, ethics boards and (for X) the European Commission's supervision may all want them.

Keeping this guide current

Platform programs change monthly. Reliable trackers: the DSA Observatory, the Weizenbaum Institute data access papers, the DSA40 Data Access Collaboratory, and each platform's developer changelog. This guide was fully verified in October 2026, and the site is monitored with Claude: changes are checked and verified against official sources before they appear here.
